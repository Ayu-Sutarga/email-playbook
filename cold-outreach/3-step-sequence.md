# The 3-Step Cold Sequence: Authority → Proof → Soft Close

A sequence for introducing an expert (speaker, consultant, coach, author) to a decision-maker who doesn't know them yet: HR leaders, event producers, speaker bureaus.

**Timing:** Email 1 → wait 3–4 days → Email 2 → wait 4–5 days → Email 3

**The logic:** each email *lowers the ask*. A stranger won't say yes to a big commitment, but they might say yes to something small, and small yeses build trust.

| Email | Job | Ask |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Show the problem + why this expert is credible | 15-minute call |
| 2 | Show proof + who it's for | Send an outline *or* call (a choice) |
| 3 | Make it easy | A short, low-commitment session |

---

## Email 1: Primary outreach (Awareness + Authority)

**Subject options**
- `{{firstName}}, a different lens on your {{YEAR}} {{TOPIC}} agenda`
- `The {{PAIN_POINT}} problem nobody's solving`

```
{{RANDOM|Hi|Hello|Hey}} {{firstName}},

You know this challenge better than anyone: {{STAT_ABOUT_PAIN_POINT}}. Your teams are feeling it as {{SYMPTOM}}.

In this environment, {{TOPIC}} isn't a soft skill. It's {{REFRAME}}.

{{EXPERT_NAME}} is {{ONE_LINE_POSITIONING}}.

- Reach: {{AUDIENCE_NUMBER}} people reached in {{TIMEFRAME}}
- Proof: {{EVIDENCE_OR_METHOD}}
- Credibility: {{NOTABLE_STAGE_OR_MEDIA}}

Here's a quick look at their work: {{SHOWREEL_LINK}}

Are you free for a 15-minute call next week to see if it fits your agenda?

Best,
{{YOUR_NAME}}
```

---

## Email 2: Follow-up (Proof + Relevance)

**Subject:** reply in the same thread (no new subject)

```
{{RANDOM|Hi|Hello}} {{firstName}},

Following up briefly on {{EXPERT_NAME}}'s work.

Their programmes help leaders:
- {{OUTCOME_1}}
- {{OUTCOME_2}}
- {{OUTCOME_3}}

One recent client described it as:
"{{SHORT_TESTIMONIAL}}"

It's currently used by:
- {{AUDIENCE_TYPE_1}}
- {{AUDIENCE_TYPE_2}}

To save you time, I can send a sample outline with three outcomes for {{THEIR_PRIORITY}}.

Would you prefer the outline, or a quick chat next week?

Warmly,
{{YOUR_NAME}}
```

---

## Email 3: Soft close (Low-commitment offer)

**Subject:** reply in the same thread

```
{{RANDOM|Hi|Hello}} {{firstName}},

Just one final loop.

I know a full {{BIG_OFFER}} may not be a priority right now. So many teams start smaller, with a {{SMALL_OFFER}} ({{DURATION}}) that gives them:

- {{QUICK_WIN_1}}
- {{QUICK_WIN_2}}
- {{QUICK_WIN_3}}

Minimal scheduling, immediate value.

If that sounds useful, I can check availability today.

Thank you for your time. I'm here whenever you'd like to continue the conversation.

Warm regards,
{{YOUR_NAME}}
```

---

## 🧠 Why it works
- **Email 1** opens with *their* problem, not your expert. People read what's about them.
- **Email 2** offers a **choice between two yeses** (outline *or* call), which feels easier than a yes/no.
- **Email 3** uses **lowering the ask**: if the big thing is too much, a small thing feels reasonable.
- The closing line of Email 3 leaves the door open with warmth, which protects the relationship for later.
