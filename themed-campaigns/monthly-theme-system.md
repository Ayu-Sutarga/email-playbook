# The Monthly Theme System

For keeping **agencies, bureaus or partners** warm over time. Instead of one generic "here's what we offer" email, each month focuses on **one market pain point** and shows exactly what's bookable for it.

It works because partners can only sell what they can clearly describe to *their* clients. Each email gives them a ready-made pitch.

---

## The structure (same every month)

1. **Theme**: the market pain point this month
2. **Promotes**: which product, book or programme it links to
3. **Stat hook**: open with a surprising number
4. **Proof moment**: a recent stage or event where this was delivered, plus one memorable line
5. **Credibility**: who trusts the expert
6. **Three bookable programmes**: title + one-line promise
7. **Fit line**: *"If your clients want ___, ___ is the right fit."*
8. **Easy contact**: "Simply reply to book"

## ⚠️ Rules for stat hooks
- Use **real, sourced statistics**, and keep the source handy in case a partner asks.
- Use one number per email. More than that becomes noise.
- Refresh stats yearly. Old data hurts credibility.
