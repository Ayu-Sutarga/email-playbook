# ✉️ Email Playbook

**Cold outreach, newsletters and campaign emails that land in the inbox and get replies.**

This is my working playbook from years of B2B outreach and newsletters for keynote speakers, authors and founders, built and tested in Instantly. Everything here is unbranded and ready to adapt.

What makes it different: every template comes with the **psychology behind it** (NLP, persuasion, behavioural economics) and the **deliverability thinking** that keeps it out of spam.

### 🧭 My core principles
1. **One email, one job.** One main call to action per email.
2. **Light beats heavy.** Simple HTML that reads like a person wrote it gets more inbox placement than designed blasts.
3. **Lower the ask as the sequence goes.** Meeting → resource → small, low-commitment offer.
4. **Iterate on evidence.** Rewrite in cycles based on what the replies (and silences) tell you.
5. **Track cleanly.** Consistent UTMs, or your data lies to you.

### 🔧 How to use
- Replace everything in `{{DOUBLE_BRACES}}` with your own details.
- `{{RANDOM|Hi|Hello|Dear}}` is Instantly spintax: each recipient gets one option at random.
- Paste the HTML files into Instantly's HTML editor, then **send yourself a test** before launching.
